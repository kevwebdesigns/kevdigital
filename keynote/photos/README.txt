Drop your photos in this folder using the exact file names below,
then run  npm run build  in the keynote folder to rebuild the PDF.

Any photo you don't add just stays as a labelled grey frame.

FILE NAME        SLIDE   SHAPE            WHAT IT IS
kevin.jpg        1       tall portrait    A photo of you (this is the big cover image)
campus.jpg       2       tall portrait    A college campus
studying.jpg     2       landscape        Studying / notes / laptop
business.jpg     2       landscape        Business class, notebook, whiteboard
miami.jpg        3       very wide        Miami skyline (wide banner across the top)
workspace.jpg    3       square-ish       A small office or desk setup
work.jpg         3       square-ish       Someone working / a small business
family.jpg       4       tall portrait    Family
dog.jpg          4       wide             A dog
beach.jpg        4       wide             Miami / the beach
hobby.jpg        4       very wide        Gym, friends, car - something you actually do
barcelona.jpg    5       tall portrait    Barcelona or somewhere in Spain

Photos are cropped to fill their frame from the centre, so put the important
part of the picture near the middle.
