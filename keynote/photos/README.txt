Five photos. Drop them in this folder with these exact file names,
then run  npm run build  to rebuild the PDF.

Any photo you skip just stays as a labelled grey frame.

FILE NAME       SLIDE  SHAPE          WHAT IT IS
kevin.jpg         1    tall           A photo of you - the big cover image
campus.jpg        2    square-ish     A college campus
miami.jpg         3    wide           Miami skyline (runs across the top)
family.jpg        4    square-ish     Family
barcelona.jpg     5    tall           Barcelona or somewhere in Spain

SHAPE MATTERS. Tall slots want a vertical photo, wide slots want a
horizontal one. Photos are cropped from the centre to fill their frame,
so keep the important part near the middle.
